# Lighting and ceiling concept

## Principles

1. **Keep the structure visible.** The existing steel deck and beams stay exposed (client instruction) and are painted matt warm
   white so the ceiling reflects light and the rooms feel taller.
2. **Use the beams.** Track rails are fixed to the beam soffits in the experience centre; acoustic felt rafts sit in the beam bays
   of the boardroom; felt baffles run between the beams in merchandising; suspended oak slat rafts hang just below beam level in the
   reception and MD office. Nothing is cut into the structure.
3. **Layer the light.**
   - Ambient / task: suspended linear LED profiles, direct + indirect (about 75 % down, 25 % up to the deck), over every desk row and
     meeting table, about 80 in above the floor.
   - Accent: 3000 K adjustable track spots on garments, mannequins, brand walls and the mood-board wall.
   - Feature: brushed-brass halo rings over the reception lounge, the MD lounge and the staff lounge; opal pendants in the cafe.
   - Circulation and wet areas: surface cylinder downlights. Prayer rooms: wide-beam (about 110 deg) 3000 K downlights for calm,
     even light without glare.
4. **Colour temperature by zone.** 4000 K in work areas (merchandising, commercial, HR, CAD, studio, library, sample room) for
   alertness and colour judgement; 3000-3500 K in the visitor and executive zones for warmth; 3000 K in prayer rooms.
   Colour-critical fabric checks use the D65 booth in the fabric & trim library, not the room lighting.
5. **Controls (recommended).** Scene control (DALI or similar) in the boardroom, experience centre and reception; occupancy
   sensors in washrooms, prayer rooms, store and corridors; daylight dimming for the desks beside the east windows.
