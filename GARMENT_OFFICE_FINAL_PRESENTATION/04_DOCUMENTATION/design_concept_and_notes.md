## 2. Design language - "Green Thread"

- **Base:** warm white walls, exposed steel deck and beams painted warm white (kept, as instructed), warm beige terrazzo floors in
  the visitor zone.
- **Warmth:** fluted / slatted white oak (reception, lobby brand wall, boardroom media wall, executive corridor), smoked walnut for
  the executive suite and boardroom table.
- **Brand accent:** deep green taken from the client's logo (#26673D): display-bay backs in the experience centre, boardroom
  acoustic rafts, selected upholstery and felt baffles. Used sparingly so the garments and the logo remain the focus.
- **Transparency:** slim matt-black aluminium framed glass partitions with frosted manifestation bands; the 48 in existing walls
  get glass above where views help (offices, boardroom, passage) and solid infill where display walls are needed.
- **Details:** brushed brass in small doses (halo pendants, display rails, door pulls), black metal legs and frames.
- **Branding:** only the client's own artwork (ZF Zara Fashion logo, Stage II Knitting Apparel Ltd. and Unit-1 logos, tagline)
  on white plates - lobby brand wall, reception, experience centre story wall, MD office. "Global Design Studio" appears only
  as the room name because the master plan calls it a proposed identity pending trademark checks.

## 3. Zoning and movement

- **Visitor route:** main gate - entrance lobby (security / visitor check-in, guard as instructed) - reception + waiting -
  visitor passage (guest WCs) - experience centre - boardroom - back to reception. Visitors never cross office areas.
- **Executive link:** reception - executive reception (PA + waiting) - glass executive corridor - Director / MD; direct door
  from the executive suite to the boardroom.
- **Staff route:** gate - lobby - staff door - staff corridor - HR & admin / commercial & accounts - hallway - merchandising and
  the design-development wing (CAD, fabric library, 3D lab, sample room, design studio, store) - staff cafe, lounge / training,
  prayer rooms. HR & admin also opens to the reception (existing 57 in opening) for candidates and vendors.
- **Head count basis:** company profile: about 30 management/staff; master plan: studio team of about 6. Provided: 48 dedicated
  workstations + 6 guest work seats + 15-seat boardroom + audit room for 5 - room for about 50 % growth.

## 4. Key spaces

- **Entrance lobby:** walnut security desk facing the gate; oak-slat brand wall with the three client logos and tagline on a
  white panel, lit by track spots; existing stair kept (configuration confirmed by the owner).
- **Reception + waiting:** fluted-oak reception counter with terrazzo ledge and 760 mm accessible section; backlit logo plate on
  a fluted oak wall; waiting lounge (sand sofa, green armchairs, walnut table, wool rug) under a suspended oak slat raft with a
  brass halo pendant; glass view from the lobby.
- **Experience centre (707 sft, centre of the floor):** brand-story wall (logos + tagline), four garment display bays with deep
  green backs, brass rails and LED shelves; mannequin plinth; "yarn to garment" touch table (yarn cones, fabric swatches,
  folded garments); long terrazzo display counter; digital totem; track lights fixed to the existing beams aimed at every
  display. Glass to the boardroom and the fabric library makes it the visible heart of the office.
- **Boardroom (540 sft, 15 seats):** 6.1 m walnut table with power/data trough, black leather chairs, 86 in screen on an oak slat
  media wall, walnut acoustic slats, green felt rafts set between the existing beams, linear pendants over the table.
- **MD office (300 sft):** 2.1 m walnut desk with leather pad, cognac executive chair, walnut feature wall with the logo, lounge
  corner (sofa, armchair, coffee table, rug), oak slat raft + halo, herringbone oak floor. Director office and PA + waiting
  alongside, glass fronts on an oak-lined executive corridor.
- **Merchandising open office (1,421 sft, 16 desks + 8-seat team table):** 1400 x 700 mm bench desks with green felt screens,
  sample-review counter with a pinboard (trims, sample boards) on the experience-centre wall, lockers and low storage under the
  borrowed-light windows, felt baffles between the beams, 4000 K linear pendants.
- **Commercial & accounts (12 desks + manager), HR & admin (4 desks + interview table), audit room, guest workspace:** same desk
  family and lighting for a consistent workplace.
- **Global Design Studio (936 sft) + support:** creative workstations by the east windows, 7.4 m mood-board wall (trend research),
  trend table, collection display and buyer-presentation table with 86 in screen; CAD / technical design room with plotter and
  layout table; fabric, colour & trim library with D65 colour booth; 3D printing lab; sample development room (pattern & cutting
  table, 2 sample machines, dress forms, steam station) - an office-grade sample studio, not production; sample & material store.
- **Staff support:** staff cafe + pantry (16 seats), lounge / training room (sofa group + 6-seat workshop table + screen), male
  prayer + wudu, female prayer + wudu, staff washrooms (female 2 WC; male 2 WC + 2 urinals), 2 guest WCs.

## 5. Ceilings and lighting

The beams stay exposed (client instruction) and are used as the ceiling's structure: track lights fixed to the beam soffits in
the experience centre, felt rafts set into the beam bays in the boardroom, felt baffles between the beams in merchandising,
suspended oak slat rafts below beam level in the reception and MD office. Lighting is layered: 4000 K direct/indirect linear
pendants over desks (up-light brightens the painted deck), 3000-3500 K in visitor and executive areas, 3000 K accent spots on
products and brand walls, halo pendants as focal points, downlights in circulation and washrooms. See `lighting_schedule.md` and
drawing ID-03.

## 7. Model and visualisation notes

- 3D: Blender 5.2.3, generated by Python scripts (`01_EDITABLE_3D/scripts`); every element is a separate, named object in named
  collections; furniture shares mesh data (linked duplicates) for a small file.
- Furniture: parametric procedural models with real dimensions; lounge armchairs, plants, cafe pendants and small decor are CC0
  models from Poly Haven; textures CC0 from Poly Haven / ambientCG (see `01_EDITABLE_3D/assets/LICENSES.md`).
- Renders: Cycles (CPU), AgX colour management, OpenImageDenoise. Images are visualisations of the proposal; garments, screens
  and mood boards show neutral placeholder colours, not real products.
