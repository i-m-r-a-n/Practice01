About these images
  • All 38 images are Blender 5.2 Cycles renders of this project's 3D model (01_EDITABLE_3D/Final_Office_Interior.blend), not photographs. Materials and plants use CC0 textures and models listed in 01_EDITABLE_3D/assets/LICENSES.md; no stock photos or AI-generated images are used.
  • Resolution and samples of every view are listed in 02_RENDERED_IMAGES/final/render_log.csv; the PNG files in the same folder are the lossless originals.
  • Furniture is parametric and generic (no specific manufacturer). Screens, garments, mood-board cards and book spines are neutral placeholder colours, not real products or content.
  • Views through the windows show a sky model, not the real surroundings.
  • Floor-to-deck height (114 in, estimated from photos), the width of room 8 and the column and beam positions are provisional until measured on site (04_DOCUMENTATION/unresolved_issues.md).
  • "Global Design Studio" is the name proposed in the master plan, used here for wayfinding only.
  • Interior design proposal - not for construction.
