Design philosophy

Six principles behind every decision

01

Modernity

Clean planes, slim black-aluminium glass, an exposed and painted structure - an honest contemporary shell.

02

Functionality

Clear zoning: visitors, executives, administration, design development and staff support each have their place.

03

Collaboration

Team tables, a buyer presentation table, a sample-review counter and glass views between departments.

04

Creativity

Mood-board wall, fabric & trim library, 3D printing lab and display bays keep products and ideas visible.

05

Employee comfort

Ergonomic 1400 mm desks, acoustic felt, 4000 K task light, staff cafe, lounge and prayer rooms.

06

Professional image

The client's own logos and green, terrazzo, oak and walnut: a confident international brand presence.

Design benefits

What the company gains

Operational efficiency

Departments placed by workflow: merchandising beside the experience centre and sample room, HR at the staff entrance, the studio next to CAD, library and samples.

Professional image

A visitor sequence that presents the brand, the product and the management in one walk.

Employee comfort

Ergonomic desks, acoustic control, daylight in the studio, cafe, lounge and prayer rooms.

Flexibility

Bench desks, a multipurpose lounge / training room, glass partitions and about 50 % seat growth.

Maintainability

Hard-wearing terrazzo and porcelain, replaceable carpet tiles, exposed services that stay accessible.

Implementation considerations

To confirm before construction

ItemWhat has to be confirmed
Site measurementsFloor-to-deck height, room 8 width, column positions and beam layout (currently estimated).
StructureLintels for the new openings D17, D20, D22 and the lobby-reception glass - structural engineer.
Fire & escapeGround-floor exits of the NE / SE stairs; without them the walk to the main gate is about 220 ft from the lounge.
MEPVRF cooling, fresh air for windowless rooms, exhausts (WCs, pantry, 3D lab), power/data, UPS, DB location.
AccessibilityRamp or lift at the two entrance steps; one accessible WC (door at least 36 in).
Approvals & brandingFire / building approvals as applicable; trademark check for 'Global Design Studio'; signage from original vector logos.

No budget or programme was supplied, so none is stated. Details: 04_DOCUMENTATION/technical_coordination_notes.md.

Recommended next steps
01

Site measurement

Floor-to-deck height, room 8 width, columns and beams; then update the survey data, rebuild and re-verify the model.

02

Engineering reviews

Structural check of the new openings, fire strategy and exits, HVAC, power and data design.

03

Client decisions

Head count per department, interior budget, experience-centre content and approval of this layout.

04

Detailed design

Construction drawings, finish samples, furniture selection and bill of quantities after approval.
