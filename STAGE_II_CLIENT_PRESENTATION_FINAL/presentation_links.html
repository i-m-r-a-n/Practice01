Mac + TV / projector checklist

  • Power adapter on; Focus / Do Not Disturb on; other apps closed.
  • Connect HDMI / USB-C. System Settings → Displays → Mirror; resolution "Default for display".
  • Files 1-6 in Downloads; ZIP unzipped (keep the renders folder next to the HTML).
  • Open the 3D tour in Firefox, close the help card, press F for full screen, try the Guided tour for 10 s, press Esc.
  • Open both videos (QuickTime) and the PDFs (Preview).
  • Display quality (? button): High; choose Balanced if movement is not smooth.
  • The videos have no sound (silent track): no audio setup needed.
  • Trackpad: drag to turn, Shift + drag to move, pinch to zoom. Esc = overview.

Fallback

  • No internet: nothing changes; all files are on the Mac.
  • 3D tour does not start: Firefox → Settings → General → Performance → "Use recommended performance settings", restart; or open the same file in Chrome or Safari.
  • 3D tour slow: ? → Display quality → Balanced or Low.
  • 3D tour fails: play the 2:01 tour video and use the render book.
  • Video does not play: drag it into Firefox or Chrome, or use the 720p copy.
  • Projector picture cut off: Displays → "Default for display" or 1920 × 1080.
  • Live website: backup only (needs internet).
Tested in Firefox 159 and Chromium (cloud machine, software GPU): 3D tour 25/25 checks, no overlapping panels at 7 Mac window sizes; videos decoded without errors or black frames. Not tested: macOS / Safari. Open the files once on the Mac before the meeting.
