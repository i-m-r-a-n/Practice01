# Run-of-show: 20-25 minute client meeting

Files (downloaded to the Mac beforehand): `Client_Pitch_Cinematic_Tour.mp4`, `Complete_Ground_Floor_Tour.mp4`,
`Garment_Office_Design_Pitch.pdf`, `Garment_Office_Render_Book.pdf`, the unzipped folder `Ground_Floor_3D_Tour_Mac`.
Open them before the client arrives: videos in QuickTime, PDFs in Preview, the 3D tour in Firefox (help card closed).

| Time | Step | What to show | What to say (short) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1. Cinematic introduction | `Client_Pitch_Cinematic_Tour.mp4`, full screen (QuickTime: View -> Enter Full Screen) | "This is the proposed ground floor, rendered from the project's 3D model." |
| 1:30 | 2. Architectural vision | Pitch PDF slides 1-3 (title, project vision, design philosophy) | The six design principles; premium international buyer experience. |
| 3:30 | 3. Floor plan and zoning | Pitch PDF slides 4-5 (plans) | Arrival, experience and meeting, executive, workplace, design development, support zones. |
| 6:00 | 4. Complete 3D model | 3D tour: Overview -> **Isometric**; **Ceilings** off; drag to turn | "The whole floor, furnished, in real-time 3D." Click a room on the model to show how it works. |
| 7:30 | 5. Interactive tour | 3D tour: **Guided tour** (gold button). Press **Space** to pause at any stop for questions, **->** next, **<-** back | The tour plays by itself through 16 stops in 3D. |
| 8:30 | 6. Reception | tour stop / or Rooms -> Reception & visitor waiting; drag to look around | Fluted-oak counter, backlit logo wall, waiting lounge. |
| 10:00 | 7. Experience Centre | Rooms -> Experience centre | Where buyers see the product; display and sampling. |
| 11:30 | 8. Boardroom | Rooms -> Boardroom | Long table, acoustic rafts, glazed wall to the experience centre. |
| 13:00 | 9. Executive offices | Rooms -> Managing Director, Director, Executive reception | Executive suite and its own reception. |
| 14:30 | 10. Merchandising and Design Studio | Rooms -> Merchandising; Rooms -> Global Design Studio; optional **Walk** (W A S D) between them | Open workplaces; design development wing. |
| 16:30 | 11. Photoreal finishes | Render book PDF (key rooms) or the room card's **Photoreal render** button | "These stills are path-traced from the same model: the closest view of the final finishes." |
| 18:30 | 12. Materials and lighting | Pitch PDF slides 14-15 (materials and finishes, plan) | Warm durable palette; lighting concept. |
| 20:00 | 13. Implementation | Pitch PDF slide 17 (implementation considerations) | Items to confirm before construction: site survey of dimensions, structure, MEP, fire, approvals. |
| 21:30 | 14. Approval request | Pitch PDF slides 16 and 18 (benefits, closing) | Ask for approval of the concept and the layout as the basis for the next stage. |
| 23:00 | Optional recap | `Complete_Ground_Floor_Tour.mp4` (2:01) | - |

Say clearly when asked: this is a **concept proposal**; dimensions are site-estimated and subject to verification; the real-time
3D is simpler than the Cycles renders (no reflections, softer light); no structural, MEP or authority approvals exist yet.

## Checklist: Apple Silicon Mac + TV or projector (one page)

Before the client arrives (15 minutes):
- [ ] Power adapter connected; battery saver off; Do Not Disturb / Focus on; notifications off.
- [ ] Close other apps and browser tabs (Firefox should be the only heavy app).
- [ ] Connect the TV / projector (HDMI / USB-C adapter). System Settings -> Displays: choose **Mirror** (same picture on
      both), resolution "Default for display" (1920 x 1080 is fine). Check the picture fills the screen.
- [ ] Sound: the videos have no music or narration (a silent audio track); no sound setup needed.
- [ ] Files in Downloads: the two videos, the pitch PDF, the render book PDF, the unzipped `Ground_Floor_3D_Tour_Mac` folder.
- [ ] Open `Ground_Floor_3D_Tour.html` in Firefox (right-click -> Open With -> Firefox). Wait for the model; close the help
      card; press **F** for full screen; try Guided tour for 10 seconds, then press **Esc** (back to overview).
- [ ] Open both videos in QuickTime and both PDFs in Preview; leave them open in the Dock.
- [ ] Display quality in the tour (**?** button): High on Apple Silicon; if movement is not smooth, choose Balanced.

During the meeting:
- Switch apps with **Cmd + Tab**. In the tour: **Esc** = back to overview, **Space** = pause, **<-  ->** = stops.
- Trackpad: drag to turn, **Shift + drag** to move, pinch / scroll to zoom.

## Fallback (if something fails)

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| No internet / Wi-Fi | Nothing changes: everything above runs from the Mac's own files. |
| Live website broken or slow | Do not use it; the offline tour (`Ground_Floor_3D_Tour.html`) is the same tour with the improved model. |
| 3D tour does not start in Firefox | Firefox -> Settings -> General -> Performance: tick "Use recommended performance settings" and restart Firefox; or open the same file in Chrome or Safari. |
| 3D tour too slow on the projector | **?** -> Display quality -> Balanced or Low; or leave full screen. |
| 3D tour fails completely | Play `Complete_Ground_Floor_Tour.mp4` (2:01) and use the render book PDF for room-by-room discussion. |
| Video does not play | Open it in Firefox or Chrome (drag the file onto the window), or use the 720p copy. |
| Projector picture cut off | System Settings -> Displays -> choose "Default for display" or 1920 x 1080. |
