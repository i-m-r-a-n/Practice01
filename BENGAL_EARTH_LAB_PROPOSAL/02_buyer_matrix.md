# Bengal Earth Lab — European buyer requirements & first outreach matrix

**Research checked:** 30 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). **Status:** completed desk-research matrix, NOT verified buyer demand, legal opinion, supplier qualification or an export order.

[Editable source: 02_buyer_matrix.csv](02_buyer_matrix.csv) covers **9 destination markets × 8 buyer segments = 72 labelled targeting hypotheses**. Each row includes product-focus hypothesis, proposed price positioning, buyer-data gaps, country regulatory gate, packaging, sustainability evidence requests, purchase/rejection risks and one **officially announced** trade event with URL/date. **No individual buyer, MOQ, quoted lead time, wholesale price, importer name or demand volume was invented.**

## Executive decision: sell a system, not a souvenir

The differentiated offer should be an original **Bengal Earth Lab collection-development programme**: a coordinated set of natural-material samples, fully dimensioned and repeatable designs, careful making/finishing, origin evidence, price quotation inputs, safety dossiers and documented replacement/repair methods. Retail-shelf product prices in [01_retail_benchmark.csv](01_retail_benchmark.csv) are *not* evidence of a feasible factory export price or retailer margin.

**Suggested first testing routes (not confirmed demand):**
1. **Independent boutiques + premium lifestyle brands:** P01 woven nesting storage, P03 protective terracotta planter sleeve, original handloom textiles. Request assortment feedback using actual samples; do not claim artisan or local raw origin before verification.
2. **Fair-trade outlets:** maker-led collection developed under community agreement, documented pay/consent and truthful material provenance. Do not display an invented certification or use sacred/community-restricted motifs.
3. **Interior designers + hospitality:** tested removable panels and repairable accent furniture. They are not contract-grade until tests and application-specific fire/installation checks are completed.

Large chains and department stores become sensible targets only if repeatability, quality-control, packing and batch traceability are demonstrable. This is a **risk-managed route recommendation**, not a claim about their published sourcing plans.

### MOQ, lead time, wholesale pricing — red-line handling

**No named purchaser's published MOQ or lead time** was established in the reviewed official market and event sources, so the CSV explicitly says *Not found in cited public sources; buyer confirmation needed*. Do not substitute invented 50/100/500-pc MOQs, 30/45/60-day production times or FOB rates. To prepare a genuine quote, ask for item/drawing version, tested sample status, desired quantity per SKU, packaging, destination, Incoterms, target inspection/AQL, shipping window and payment terms. Provide a cost template once real workshop quotes exist.

### Packaging and labelling

For import cartons, prepare a **buyer-confirmed** label/packing list recording producer, consignee, product/SKU, quantities, gross/net mass, dimensions, warnings and handling, with carton identifiers. CBI presents these as buyer/importer logistics practices—not a universal legal claim that all cartons in Europe need the same barcode. For consumer textiles covered by EU law, fibre content labels are mandatory in applicable official market languages; GB has distinct textile guidance. EU packaging product-operator obligations under PPWR started applying **12 August 2026**, with later measures phased in. Do not use an unverified all-material-recyclable claim. Sources: [CBI](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/home-decoration-textiles/buyer-requirements), [EU textiles](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/textile-products-textile-fibre-names-and-labelling.html), [EU Commission packaging, 11 Aug 2026](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/new-eu-rules-packaging-enter-application-2026-08-11_en), [UK textile guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/textile-labelling).

### Sustainability — do not merge requests with law

A buyer may request Sedex / SMETA, amfori BSCI, ETI, Fairtrade, WFTO or other schemes, depending on its procurement model. **No buyer in this study has been identified as requiring any particular scheme**, and Bengal Earth Lab is not claimed enrolled, audited or certified. CBI discusses these as voluntary buyer expectations; establish exact applicability and evidence on the actual SKU and purchase order. Claims such as `100% Bangladesh-made`, `solar-powered`, `recycled`, `chemical-free` or `fair trade` need substantiation; a narrative is not proof.

## Product safety: legal gates that cannot be inferred from beautiful renders

**EU27 (Germany, Netherlands, France, Sweden, Denmark, Belgium, Italy, Spain):**
- Assess **Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR)** applicability to each non-harmonised consumer product; document manufacturer/responsible-person details and safety/traceability including online offers where relevant. Never infer that a generic furniture item needs CE marking or that all non-electrical decor is exempt. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj/eng.
- Assess **EU textile fibre names/labelling** for in-scope textiles (composition, languages and material-content claims); don't declare a woven product `100% jute` without composition evidence. Official summary: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/textile-products-textile-fibre-names-and-labelling.html.
- **Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation** rules apply from 12 August 2026 with phased dates; importer/producer duties must be allocated to the real operator, not guessed. Official Commission news: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/new-eu-rules-packaging-enter-application-2026-08-11_en.
- Product-specific checks, still **to verify by SKU**: restricted substances, ceramic food contact, wood products, furniture strength/fire and relevant environmental claims. This matrix does **not** certify compliance or list every law.

**United Kingdom:** GOV.UK provides separate GB general consumer product safety, textile labelling and furniture fire-safety guidance. **Northern Ireland applies relevant EU product-safety requirements including EU GPSR**, so confirm destination rather than writing `UK = EU` or `UK = GB`. Only apply UK upholstered-furniture fire rules to items actually in scope; the 2025 amendment took effect 30 October 2025. Primary sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/product-safety-advice-for-businesses, https://www.gov.uk/guidance/textile-labelling, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/furniture-and-furnishings-fire-safety-regulations, and [NI/EU guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eu-regulation-2023988-on-general-product-safety/eu-regulation-on-general-product-safety-2023988).
