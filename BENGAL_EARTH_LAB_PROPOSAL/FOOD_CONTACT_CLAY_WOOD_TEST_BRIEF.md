# Bengal Earth Lab — EU and UK food-contact gate for clay and wooden tableware
**Research checked: 30 October 2026. Status: draft specification, NOT a laboratory report, certificate, legal sign-off or commercial-release approval.** No Bangladesh maker, wood species, glaze composition, laboratory, shipment or test results have been verified.

## Scope / mandatory release questions
For *each* cup, mug, bowl, plate, spoon, tray, wooden plate or other foreseeable food-contact article, document: destination (specific EU member state, England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland), intended food types, acidic/fatty contact, hot/cold temperatures, contact duration, repeated use, washing, dishwasher/microwave/oven claims, all food-contact finishes and joining materials, and lot/batch ID. A decorative-only label is not a workaround for foreseeable use as tableware.

**All food-contact materials in the EU:** Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 Articles 3, 15, 17 require inert/safe foreseeable use, accurate labelling and traceability; Commission Regulation (EC) 2023/2006 requires documented GMP/QA/QC. The European Commission confirms that national rules may apply where no specific harmonised EU measure exists. [S1–S3]

**Fired ceramic for the EU:** Directive 84/500/EEC as amended by 2005/31/EC sets **finished-product** lead and cadmium migration (release) limits, testing rules and requirements for written ceramic declarations and supporting analysis. A glaze formula described as “lead free” does not prove release from the finished item. [S4–S5]

| EU ceramic category under Article 2(4) | Pb max | Cd max |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 — unfillable or internal depth ≤25 mm | 0.8 mg/dm² | 0.07 mg/dm² |
| 2 — other fillable objects | 4.0 mg/L | 0.3 mg/L |
| 3 — cooking ware, or packaging/storage vessels >3 litres | 1.5 mg/L | 0.1 mg/L |

The Directive sets additional rules for ceramic lids and a conditional multiple-sample assessment in Article 2(5); **do not treat a failed single result as an automatic pass**. [S4]

**Ceramic laboratory request:** verify the actual product category and test finished, representative worst-case decoration/glaze samples. Directive Annex I calls for fresh **4% (v/v) aqueous acetic acid at 22 ±2 °C for 24 ±0.5 h**, with the specified light/dark and surface/filling rules. Annex II (amended 2005/31) sets instrumental performance requirements. Ask the lab to return sample IDs, raw results, units, category/limits, method, test conditions, analytical detection/quantification, deviations, lab name/address, dates and responsible signatory. Specify how separately tested ceramic lids are accounted for. A test certificate for raw clay or unfired glaze is not equivalent. [S4–S5]

**Great Britain is not identical to EU/NI:** the amended England *Materials and Articles in Contact with Food Regulations 2012* include ceramic limits, Schedules 2–4 for migration testing and a **GB-form declaration** (including maker and GB importer identities, article, date and compliance). Scotland has its own 2012 rules; confirm Wales' applicable law and each market with the buyer before placing goods. **Northern Ireland** follows relevant EU food-contact rules under Windsor arrangements; UK FSA's 25 June 2026 BPA guidance distinguishes EU/NI from GB. Do not copy an EU declaration template unchanged as a GB declaration. [S6–S9]

**Food-contact wood:** the EU Commission's list of harmonised material-specific measures does not include a blanket EU measure for untreated wood. General FCM/GMP law, destination-country rules, and case-specific chemistry of applied oils, wax, lacquers, adhesives, pigments and liners still apply. **There is no universal wood “food-safe” migration threshold established by this brief.** Obtain full formulation and process data, then a competent lab's written risk-based selection of analytes, simulants and exposure conditions before testing; investigate harmful transfer, taint/odour, coating wear, finish breakdown, cleaning, splinters, cracking and moisture hygiene. “Natural” and “non-toxic” marketing is not compliance documentation. [S1–S3]

**Coating-sensitive EU/NI substances:** identify whether coatings or adhesives trigger Regulation (EU) 2024/3190 on BPA and other bisphenols, as corrected in 2026/250, including its product-specific transitions. Do not assume it applies identically to all uncoated timber. FSA says EU BPA changes do not automatically apply in GB. [S9, S12]

**Reform status at 30 October 2026:** the Commission is evaluating lower Pb/Cd limits and additional metals for ceramic/vitreous materials, with a stakeholder event scheduled 25 November 2026. Those contemplated tighter numbers are **not represented here as enacted limits**. Re-check consolidated law immediately before commercial release. [S10–S11]
