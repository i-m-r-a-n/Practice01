# European retail benchmark — tranche 1 (source-linked retailer listings)

**Checked on:** 30 October 2026. **Status:** PARTIAL — 45 retailer/country listing rows towards a 120+ target. These are **consumer shelf prices**, not factory costs, wholesale quotes, confirmed orders or actual buyer interest. A market price does not establish an attainable export price.

## Method and evidence limits

- All 45 rows in [01_retail_benchmark.csv](01_retail_benchmark.csv) cite a **specific official retailer product page** and the publicly displayed price or size-dependent price range retrieved in web research on 30 October 2026. Search results were cached/crawled within approximately the preceding month in many cases; prices and availability can change. **Stock/checkout was not independently tested.**
- Markets currently sampled: **United Kingdom, Netherlands and Germany**. Retailers/catalogue fronts: **IKEA, H&M Home including ARKET listings, and Zara Home**. These are **not representative** of all nine target European countries, price bands or buyer segments. Counting the same IKEA article in separate market listings does not imply a different product.
- Row identifiers are stable design comparison IDs; Zara multiple-size prices are **ranges**, not single-variant prices. Sale prices are recorded as the retailer displayed them at lookup; normal prices noted but not substituted.
- `country_of_origin_if_stated` is **not stated on the inspected listing** for these records. Do not infer source countries, competing countries, labour practices, handmade origin or Bangladesh suitability from a brand name.
- Where dimensions are not disclosed in available indexed page text, they are **not stated**. Do not fill gaps from product photographs or another product size.
- Retailer language like `food contact safe` is a claim about that specific retailer's product, **not** evidence for a Bengal Earth Lab ceramic or wood product. All proposed food-contact prototypes need appropriate tests and documented compliant finishes.

## Specific observations grounded in the linked listings

1. **Storage systems:** repeat shelf-module dimensions appear at IKEA, including water-hyacinth/seagrass baskets around **32–35 cm** wide/deep (I04–I06), and compact woven organisers (H08). This suggests exploring **modular storage geometry**, but buyer acceptance of a Bangladeshi version remains untested.
2. **Dry-use limits matter:** IKEA's TÄTING explicitly excludes bathrooms/high humidity, while LABBSAL advises a dry, cool environment. Design product-care labels and moisture tests; do not assume water-hyacinth or grass baskets are suitable for wet bathrooms.
3. **Materials are often mixed:** e.g. TÄTING uses a steel inner frame; LABBSAL combines water hyacinth with rattan, timber and felt; H&M's seagrass laundry basket includes an iron frame. A '100% Bangladeshi-produced' promise therefore requires evidence for **every component**, not merely the outer fibre.
4. **Neutral natural tones dominate this deliberately selected sample**, with exceptions such as green terracotta and dark-blue tableware (H23/H26/H27). The sample was selected by material keywords; it does **not** prove that all European buyers prefer beige or green.
5. **Low-priced and premium-price examples coexist** (I01 vs H05 or Z05; I02 vs H17). Competing only on lowest displayed retail price is not a sound export strategy without sourced costs, buyer segment and channel margins.
6. **Food-contact distinction:** the retailer's terracotta jug listing H27 describes it as food-contact safe. Bengal Earth Lab's wood/clay cups and plates should be **on hold** until actual materials, glazes/coatings, migration testing and target-market requirements are verified.

## What still prevents a buyer-ready competitive matrix

- Need **75 more** distinct source-linked listings to reach 120 rows, with premium/fair-trade producers and more EU markets, not just UK mass retail.
- Need producer-country information **as explicitly stated** by seller/producer, not guessed from supply-chain reputation.
- Need selected buyers' live enquiry/technical packs, labelling/packaging requirements, sample QC results, real Bangladesh BOM and production quotes; retail sticker prices alone do not establish MOQ, lead time or FOB.
- SKU availability remains unverified beyond a published product page; recheck prices and exact variants on the day of buyer presentation.

## Source discipline

Every row's `URL` is the official product page used to record the facts in that row. The list is a **small evidence slice**, not a complete independent audit of every retailer's product catalogue. The attached matrix has no fictional SKUs, costs, country-of-origin claims, certifications, lead times or supplier commitments. Next research pass should add **Ferm Living, John Lewis, Bloomingville, Nkuku and fair-trade retailers** plus EU markets beyond Germany/Netherlands, preserving the same evidence standard.
