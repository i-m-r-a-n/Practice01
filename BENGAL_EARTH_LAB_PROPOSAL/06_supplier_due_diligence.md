# 06 — Bangladesh artisan, material and supplier leads (due-diligence register)

**Research date:** 30 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). **Status:** 20 evidence-linked leads; NO outreach, endorsement, contract, order, site visit, material-origin validation, food-contact test, or capacity audit. This is a search shortlist, not an approved vendor list.

**Editable machine-readable register:** [06_supplier_shortlist.csv](06_supplier_shortlist.csv). Every record includes its source URL, what that source actually says, an intentionally narrow public-evidence status, and a follow-up risk.

## 1. What the status means

- **verified exists (19):** a current publicly accessible official organisation/site, trade body, credible WFTO member page, or named-programme description was found. This **only corroborates an identifiable public lead**. It does **not** establish current manufacturing availability, financial/legal standing, authenticity of claimed raw-material origin, product safety, fair-wage delivery for a particular order, export-readiness, certification of individual products, or maker consent.
- **unverified (1):** a reported instrument-maker lead appears in an **April 2018** article only. It may no longer operate. No phone number, maker name permission, or commercial capacity has been verified.
- **Referral/intermediary is not supplier:** five entries should only help locate individual makers, clarify rights or check legality. Their presence must never be counted as an actual purchase-ready manufacturer.
- WFTO's *current member-page status* is independent organisation-level evidence for Prokritee, Dhaka Handicrafts, CORR and BaSE. WFTO membership is **not** automatically a transferable product label, origin certificate, EU conformity assessment or evidence that this project may use the logo. BaSE's separate WFTO brand profile says “Renewal in Progress”; request current enterprise/brand status before any claim [S1–S5].

## 3. Real gaps — explicitly **not solved**

1. **Direct Murta/Shital Pati seller:** UNESCO identifies the greater Sylhet weaving practice and community cooperative trend [S6]. It does not provide authorisation for the use of an individual maker's work; no directly verified maker cooperative and current procurement terms were found. Use NCCB/ECOTA as potential future introduction routes, not a fictional named co-op.
2. **Chakma and Marma suppliers:** WEAVE is a community NGO with reported traditional-knowledge work [S7], not a direct representative/contractual supplier of all Chakma or Marma weavers. The conservation programme publicly describes **Mro** artisans [S8]. Treat separate communities distinctly.
3. **Legal domestic stone producer:** the official mineral bureau explains concession controls [S9]. No licensed granite/stone dealer with quarry-to-invoice evidence was verified in this batch. No Bangladesh raw-stone claim can pass.
4. **Actual local wooden food tableware:** several sales pages advertise wood, but none establishes food-contact compliance, wood species chain, finish chemistry or batch tests for the target EU/GB market. Hold all food use; decorative samples may be explored separately. Read [FOOD_CONTACT_CLAY_WOOD_TEST_BRIEF.md](FOOD_CONTACT_CLAY_WOOD_TEST_BRIEF.md).
5. **Raw origin of electrical/solar and specialist hardware:** unknown; never classify domestic installation of imported components as “100% Bangladesh-made”.
6. **Supplier-specific samples, capacity and cost:** no direct emails/visits, audits, sample purchases, MOQs or lead-time agreements. All are open.

## 4. Evidence pack required before selecting a maker

A prospective maker must voluntarily provide, and the team must verify, a **product-specific** record rather than relying on a website:
1. Organisation registration/address, actual workshop and named responsible contact; business eligibility as needed.
2. SKU, version, original design/IP owner, written permission to feature names/photos/processes; for living heritage and Indigenous culture, community-specific free, prior, sustained and informed consent [S10–S11].
3. Bill of materials with **four separate country-of-origin stages**: raw material, processing, component manufacture and final assembly; invoice/batch or farm/forest evidence, including coatings, thread, hardware and packaging.
4. Maker/worker pay, safety protections, working hours, no coercion/child-labour process; separate independent verification where available.
5. Dated prototype, capacity limits supported by evidence, tolerances, repair plan, product-specific tests and agreed acceptance criteria.
6. Lab test accreditation and *actual SKU/lot* reports where required (especially clay/wood food-contact; never accept “food safe” website adjectives as a pass).
7. Responsible export and packaging evidence for the product's actual EU country versus Great Britain/Northern Ireland destination.
8. Quoted terms only after actual supplier quote, with currency, minimum, lead time, incoterm and sample approval. **No cost assumptions or outreach made here.**

Record accepted data in [07_material_passport_schema.json](07_material_passport_schema.json); mark unknowns as “to verify” and commercial gate HOLD. **WFTO product-logo usage must follow the organisation's own licensing/label guidance [S12].**
