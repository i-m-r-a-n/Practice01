## Approval checklist before publication
- Correct image and attribution; cultural/IP consent and fair collaboration documentation are present.
- Component origins are separated into raw materials, processing, manufacturing and assembly with source evidence.
- Product measurements, use/care, quality and regulatory status match the actual prototype revision.
- For genuine food-contact goods, a qualified product-specific EU/GB/NI test and declaration review is complete. In other cases preserve safety and any applicable product-specific rules.
- Claims about origin, worker welfare, environmental impact, certifications and solar operation have documentary support; marketing does not turn proposals into verified facts.
- Buyer-facing prices, capacity, MOQ and lead times remain blank or “quote pending” until **real** approved commercial offers exist.
