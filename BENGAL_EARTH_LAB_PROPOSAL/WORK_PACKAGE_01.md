# Bengal Earth Lab — work package 01

Prepared: 30 October 2026, Asia/Dhaka. Status: RESEARCH + DESIGN PROPOSAL, not an approved design, surveyed plan, procurement specification or deployed release.

## 1. What this package actually does

Defines a first product-development batch, preliminary existing-room programme mapping, Bangladesh-origin evidence register, furniture concepts, planting acceptance criteria and solar-evidence requirements. These are concrete starting specifications for coordinated design work, not another generic master prompt. No model geometry, approved asset, Claude branch or live tour is changed.

Owner ambition: a memorable Bengali-culture factory/design destination linking rural cottage crafts, newcomer education and European buyers. Interiors and furniture should use Bangladesh-produced materials; earth and greenery should be experienced throughout. Solar-only operation is an owner objective/statement awaiting system evidence, not a verified claim in this package.

## 2. Evidence-led commercial direction

**Verified market guidance:** CBI's basketry entry study recommends differentiation through design, craft and product stories in the middle and upper-middle segments rather than competing solely with mass-production pricing [S1]. Its February 2026 HDHT study advises segment-led targeting because European importers re-export, while noting consumer-price pressure [S2].

**Our proposed positioning:** an original Bengali collection-development partner with repeatable specifications, accountable production and credible provenance. Begin with a small coordinated collection, not an untested catalogue of 50 supposedly export-ready products. Germany, Netherlands, France and Nordic markets are research targets, not confirmed customers. No buyer interviews, orders, quotations, MOQ or prices have been obtained.

### First commercial hypotheses to validate

| Buyer segment | Proposed offer | Evidence needed before claiming readiness |
|---|---|---|
| Design-led home/lifestyle importers | Coherent basket, textile and planter-cover families | Buyer range feedback; approved sample; repeatability; packaging and costing |
| Boutique interiors/hospitality sourcing | Original Bengali furniture and display objects | Commercial-use performance; maintenance; replacement parts; installer review |
| Responsible-sourcing brands | Traceable artisan collaborations and material records | Workshop verification; contracts/payment evidence; material batch trail; relevant independent tests |

No universal European preference for a colour, price or size is assumed. Treat palettes and dimensions as options to test. Keep EU and UK compliance research separate.

### Common prototype record

Record owner, drawing/version, dimensions and tolerances, bill of materials, material-origin evidence, maker, finish system, hazards, sample photographs, test method/results, defects, repair method and next decision. Costing requires real labour/material/packaging quotes; no invented FOB prices or production capacity. Test protocols and pass limits must be selected for the product and target market with competent specialists. Internal screening is not certification.

## 4. Bangladesh-origin register — initial due-diligence queue

A local shop invoice does not establish local raw-material origin. For each bill-of-material line record raw origin, processing country, component manufacture, final assembly, supplier evidence and verification status. This is a project provenance policy, not a customs-origin determination.

| Material family | Potential use | Present evidence/status | Required follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jute and woven grass | Storage, screens and sample organisation | Candidate category, not a verified project supplier | Fibre identity, farm/source chain, yarn/weave/finish location and batch records |
| Shital Pati / Murta craft | Protected replaceable inserts | UNESCO identifies the Sylhet weaving tradition [S3]; project producer unselected | Commission with artisan consent; verify material source and fit-for-use finish |
| Jamdani craft | Display textile and selected accents | UNESCO identifies the traditional weaving practice [S4]; no project product verified | Attribute and compensate collaborators; separately verify yarn origin and composition |
| Terracotta / ceramic | Planters and tactile features | Candidate material; supplier unselected | Clay/body and glaze origin, workshop, firing consistency and application tests |
| Bamboo / cane | Screens and non-critical prototypes | Candidate only; species/source unverified | Legal sourcing, local harvest/processing, drying/treatment and durability evidence |
| Timber / engineered board | Furniture frames and work surfaces | Candidate only; domestic fabrication not proof of domestic raw timber | Legal source, traceable species/input origin, emissions and joinery performance |
| Textile offcuts | Folios and selected covers | Candidate circular route, not verified recycled content | Waste-stream custody, sorting, fibre identification and contamination controls |
| Metal / glass / aluminium | Frames, liners and durable details | Local manufacture and raw origin must be assessed separately | Manufacturer declarations and traceable inputs; flag imported primary materials |
| Adhesives / coatings / hardware | All assemblies | Often overlooked; no local-origin assumption | Full component BOM, safety documentation, performance and origin evidence |
| PV / batteries / inverters / electronics | Solar system and displays | No system selection or origin verified | Electrical-engineer assessment and owner decision on any unavailable domestic option |

UNESCO recognition of a cultural practice is not certification of our products, suppliers, environmental performance or commercial-use suitability. Do not label generic pattern printing as authentic Jamdani weaving.

## 7. Greenery: design acceptance criteria

Nature should be visible throughout, but literal planting in every corner is not a requirement. Preserve egress, sightlines, clean production, electrical clearances and maintenance access. These are project risk controls; professional design review is still required.

For each planting location record available daylight, proposed species and nursery evidence, mature size, pot/soil/water weight, water containment, drain strategy, cleaning access, maintenance owner and replacement path. Use protected soil/irrigation zones away from samples/electrical equipment. Check local nursery supply rather than calling every familiar plant native.

Prioritise daylight-edge planters and framed green views over a large unmaintainable living wall. Evaluate artificial grow-light energy before specifying low-light planting. Employees and trainees should benefit from the same quality of landscape as buyers. Plants are not a substitute for ventilation or process extraction. Species selection is not complete in this package.

## 8. Solar evidence: do not let the display outrun the system

EPA distinguishes hourly electricity matching from annual-volume matching [S5]. That distinction supports our claim discipline; the EPA page is not a Bangladesh electrical design code or proof about this site.

**Current status:** no inspected electricity bills, interval meter data, PV drawings, battery logs, installed capacity, roof survey or process-fuel inventory. Therefore 'the entire factory operates only on solar' is UNVERIFIED.

Required inputs: exact site/floor boundary; operating schedule; equipment/load inventory; interval demand; generation readings; grid import/export; storage charge/discharge and charging source; backup use; non-electric fuels; roof/shading/structure survey; single-line diagram and competent engineering review.

Dashboard schema: timestamp + timezone, meter ID/boundary, current kW, period kWh, PV generation, total load, grid import/export, storage charging/discharging and state of charge, backup contribution, data freshness and evidence link. Avoid double-counting solar at generation and again at battery discharge. Missing data displays 'Unavailable'; demonstrations display 'Simulation—not live readings'.

Do not select a battery size from annual energy alone or imply a normal grid-tied installation operates in an outage without checking the system. No final system design is provided here. Separate measured outcomes, design targets and unverified owner-supplied statements. Public wording for now: **Designed for a Solar-Powered Future**.
