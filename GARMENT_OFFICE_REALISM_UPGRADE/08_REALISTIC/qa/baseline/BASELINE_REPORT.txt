ACTUAL VISUAL REVIEW — SIX REQUESTED ROOMS
Reception / CAM_01_Reception_A: framing aligns with Cycles. Live furniture and counter are flatter/darker; contact shadow below furniture and the warm ceiling falloff are weaker. Floor highlights are less convincing.
Experience Centre / CAM_04_Experience_Centre_A: same dress forms, garments and circulation. Live ceiling and walls are over-uniform/bright compared with Cycles; dress-form and platform contacts lack depth; floor highlight is broad and poorly localized.
Boardroom / CAM_09_Boardroom_A: same room and furniture placement. Live table/chairs read dark and uniform; Cycles shows richer material response, glass/floor reflections and under-table contact. Screen appearance differs (live white, reference green) and must be reviewed as a material/light mismatch, not geometry improvement.
MD Office / CAM_07_MD_Office_A: approved framing and furniture remain. Live chairs/desk are much greyer, ceiling too evenly bright, and wall spot-light pools from Cycles are weak/absent. Floor reflection lacks the reference definition.
Merchandising / CAM_11_Merchandising_A: workstation/ceiling layout aligns. Live seating is much darker and under-desk contact is weak; desk surfaces are uniform with little daily-use detail. Reference has stronger tonal separation across the room.
Design Studio / CAM_13_Design_Studio_A: layout/camera aligns. Live table/chairs are dark and flat, garment colours muted, wall display differs, and warm indirect lighting/contact depth is reduced.
All six comparisons were actually viewed in the contact sheet. Reception source and reference were additionally inspected individually.
