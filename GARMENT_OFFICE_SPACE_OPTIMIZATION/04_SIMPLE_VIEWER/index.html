How to use this tour

  • Start guided tour plays the presentation by itself.
  • Rooms opens the room menu. Choose a room and you are taken inside.
  • Inside a room, drag to look around and scroll to zoom. Double-click or Reset view returns to the original view.
  • Floor plan shows the whole floor from above. Click any room on it.
  • Optional keys: → / ← next / previous stop, Space pause, Esc exit, H overview.
