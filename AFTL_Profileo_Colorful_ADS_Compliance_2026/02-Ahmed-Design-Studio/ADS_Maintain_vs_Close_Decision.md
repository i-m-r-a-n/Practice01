# 7. Ahmed Design Studio — Maintain vs Close Decision Checklist

**Rule: Ekhoni close korben na.** Age loan comparison, tarpor decision.

## Decision Tree

```
Step 1: Loan eligibility check (IDLC/IPDC/City Bank/BRAC)
   │
   ├── ADS route better (faster/cheaper/easier)?
   │      └── YES → Option A: Keep active, loan nin, loan cholakalin full compliance maintain
   │
   ├── AFTL route better / ADS loan possible na?
   │      └── Option B or C (niche)
   │
Step 2: Compliance cost check
   │
   ├── ADS rakhle bochore ki khoroch? (trade license renewal + VAT return + tax impact)
   │      ├── Cost kom + future use possible → Option B: Keep minimal
   │      └── Cost beshi + kono use nai → Option C: Stop-use & close safely
   │
Step 3: Long-term
   └── Option D: Profileo/Colorful/import/LC — shob AFTL-e (always)
```

## Options

### Option A — Keep active for loan
- Loan terms bhalo hole ADS diye SME loan
- Loan cholakalin: trade license + VAT + tax 100% clean rakhte hobe
- Loan purpose ADS-er business-er sathe consistent

### Option B — Keep active but minimal
- Zero/actual VAT return regular
- Trade license renewal continue
- Bank account active (minimum transaction)
- Kono notun business commitment na

### Option C — Stop-use and close safely (loan-er por / loan na lagle)
Closing sequence (kono step skip korben na):
1. [ ] Shob receivable collect / payable settle
2. [ ] VAT: pending return clear → tarpor BIN cancellation apply (VAT office)
3. [ ] Trade license: renewal bondho / surrender procedure DNCC-te jiggesh
4. [ ] Final year business income Imran-er personal return-e report
5. [ ] City Bank account: statement full download → account close (jodi ar na lage)
6. [ ] Shob record 6+ bochor archive rakhun (tax audit hole lagbe)

### Option D — Migrate to AFTL
- ADS-er kono ongoing customer/supplier thakle AFTL-e transfer letter/notice
- Notun invoice AFTL name-e
- ADS name-e notun kono contract na
