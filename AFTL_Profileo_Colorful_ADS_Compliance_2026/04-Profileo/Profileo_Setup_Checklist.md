## D. Pitch Positioning

**Problem:** Small and medium businesses in Bangladesh still use manual khata, Excel, WhatsApp, and disconnected billing systems. They cannot properly track stock, due, profit, sales, staff, purchases, suppliers, and customer history.

**Solution:** Profileo provides a Bangla-friendly cloud ERP/POS system with billing, inventory, sales, purchase, customer, due collection, quotation, invoice, reports, thermal printer, barcode/POS hardware, and business automation features.

**Target users:** hardware shops, aluminium/glass businesses, furniture hardware shops, retail/wholesale shops, importers, distributors, small factories, showrooms.

**Revenue model:** setup fee, monthly/yearly subscription, POS hardware sale, support/training, customization, cloud hosting, premium modules.
