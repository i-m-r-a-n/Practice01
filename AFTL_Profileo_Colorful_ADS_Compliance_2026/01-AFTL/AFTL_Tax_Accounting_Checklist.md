## B. Accounting Head Separation Rule

AFTL-er bhitore 3 ta division = 3 ta alada head:
1. **AFTL Core** — aluminium/glass/facade/trading
2. **Profileo** — software revenue/expense
3. **Colorful Lifestyle** — kidswear e-commerce

Prottek income/expense entry-te division tag din. Ek division-er taka arek division-e gele "inter-division transfer" hishebe likhun.
