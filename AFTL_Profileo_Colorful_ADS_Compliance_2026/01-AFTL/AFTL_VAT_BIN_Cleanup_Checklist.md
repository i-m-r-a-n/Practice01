## Step 3 — Threshold Reality Check (rules of thumb — NBR/practitioner diye confirm)

- Turnover ≤ 30 lakh → VAT payable nao lagte pare
- 30–50 lakh → turnover tax possible
- 50 lakh+ → VAT reg/payment applicable hote pare (activity-based)
- **Kintu BIN already active = return compliance alada jinish**

## Step 4 — Activity Scope Update

AFTL BIN-er activity-te thaka uchit: software/IT service, ERP/POS, SaaS, supplier, importer, trader, e-commerce, online retail, RMG/children's clothing, POS hardware, aluminium/glass/building materials.
