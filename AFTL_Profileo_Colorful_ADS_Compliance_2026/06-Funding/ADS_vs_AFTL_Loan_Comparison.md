## Known Strengths/Weaknesses (starting point)

**ADS pros:** 3-year trade license history, City Bank transaction (~50 lakh/9 months), proprietorship = simpler processing.
**ADS cons:** trade license renewal confirm korte hobe, VAT backlog possible, low closing balance (7,090), personal liability.

**AFTL pros:** limited company, long-term entity, board structure, BRAC account, future IRC/LC-er sathe align.
**AFTL cons:** trade license narrow + possibly expired, company track record kom, audited accounts chai te pare, processing slow.

## Decision Rule

1. **Speed dorkar + ADS offer reasonable** → ADS loan (Track A)
2. **AFTL offer better rate/amount + somoy ache** → AFTL loan (Track B)
3. **Dutoi weak** → age 2–3 month compliance clean + bank transaction build, tarpor abar apply
4. Kono অবস্থায় dui entity-r document mix kore ek file banaben na
