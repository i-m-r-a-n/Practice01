আগে mixed file gechilo: AFTL trade license + Imran TIN + **ADS** City Bank statement + **ADS** trade license + property photos ek sathe. Ekhon rule:

- **ADS file** = shudhu ADS trade license + ADS bank + Imran personal docs

- Dui file kokhono ek envelope/email-e na, unless bank nije দুটোই chay + explain kora hoy
