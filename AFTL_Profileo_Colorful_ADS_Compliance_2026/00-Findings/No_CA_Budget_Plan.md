## 📋 DIY Personal Tax Return Guide (Imran — zero return fix)

**Keno fix dorkar:** ager return-e income 0 dewa, kintu bank-e ~50-60 lakh/year turnover. Eta thik korte hobe — nijei eReturn-e kora jay.

Steps:
1. **eReturn login:** etaxnbr.gov.bd → TIN 315112192747 + mobile OTP. (Password bhule gele reset — nije.)
2. **Income from Business (ADS):** ekta simple income statement banan —
   - Total sales/receipts (bank deposit theke business portion — sob deposit income na, transfer/loan bad din)
   - Minus: purchase, transport, rent, utility, salary, other business cost
   - = **Net business profit** → etai business income
   - ⚠️ Sob bank deposit = income NA. Nijer/family transfer, loan, ek account theke arek account — egulo bad. Shudhu actual business sale ta income.
3. **Assets (IT-10B):** ADS business capital, bank balance (30 June), AFTL 87% share value (paid-up onujayi ~8.7 lakh), personal jinish.
4. **Expenses (IT-10BB):** family/personal yearly khoroch honestly.
5. **Tax calculate:** portal auto korbe. Taxable income tax-free limit (~3.5 lakh, NBR-e confirm) er niche hole tax 0, kintu **return ta zero-income na diye actual income diye dite hobe** — etai difference.
6. Submit → acknowledgement download।

**Note:** Ager filed return "revised" kora jay eReturn-e (assessment year select kore)। Complex mone hole ateke jaben na — je part bujhben na, screenshot diye amake jiggesh korben, ami sei field-ta explain korbo।

**Honest warning:** ami CA na, tai final tax figure ami certify korte parbo na. Kintu return **nije file kora legal ebong common** — lakh lakh manush nije eReturn-e kore. Apni parben, ami pashe achi।
