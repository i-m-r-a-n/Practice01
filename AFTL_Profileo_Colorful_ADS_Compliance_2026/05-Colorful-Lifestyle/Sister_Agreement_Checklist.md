> "Colorful Lifestyle shall operate as a brand and business division of Ahmed Facade Technologies Limited. The brand name, logo, Facebook page, website/domain, supplier relationships, customer database, product listings, stock, import documents, payment accounts, business records and all related assets shall legally belong to Ahmed Facade Technologies Limited.
>
> [Name of Sister] shall work as the Brand Manager / Operating Partner of Colorful Lifestyle and shall be responsible for product selection, supplier coordination, order management, customer communication, marketing coordination, delivery follow-up, return/exchange handling and day-to-day brand operations.
>
> For her work, she shall receive a monthly salary of BDT [amount] and/or a commission/profit share of [percentage]% based on the net profit or confirmed sales of Colorful Lifestyle, as mutually agreed in writing. All business income and expenses shall be recorded under Ahmed Facade Technologies Limited, with Colorful Lifestyle maintained as a separate accounting head.
>
> Ahmed Facade Technologies Limited shall remain the legal owner of Colorful Lifestyle, and no individual shall use the brand name, customer list, supplier list, product photos, page/domain, or business data separately without written approval from the company."
